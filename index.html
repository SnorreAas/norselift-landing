Built for the way you train

Less tapping. More lifting.

Everything in NorseLift is designed to stay out of your way between sets, and to show you exactly how you're progressing after.

01 · Log

Every set, three taps.

Your last session sits right above each exercise, with a nudge on what to beat: “+1 rep” or a little more weight. Tick a set and the rest timer starts on its own.

  • Smart suggestions: weight and reps pre-filled from your history
  • Auto rest timer: +30s or skip; vibrates on supported Android devices
  • Warm-up sets that don't pollute your stats
  • Swap exercises mid-workout without losing your plan
02 · Plan

Your week, on rails.

Build your own routines (Push/Pull/Legs, Upper/Lower, full body or anything else) and schedule them across the week. NorseLift handles the rotation, so “what am I doing today?” is always one tap away.

  • Custom programs with any exercises from a 100+ library, or your own
  • Training blocks: label a bulk, cut or peak and track how it's going
  • Cardio too: log runs, rides and more next to your lifts
03 · Progress

Your whole training picture. One page.

Your profile turns your logs into a dashboard: streak calendar, body weight, strength trend, weekly volume per muscle, records and a monthly recap. You can see at a glance whether this block is working.

  • PR celebrations at the end of every workout, free
  • Streaks & calendar that make consistency visible
  • Body weight trend alongside your lifts
  • Share card to post a finished workout
04 · Insights ⚡ PRO

Know exactly what to lift next.

Exercise Progression charts your estimated 1RM, top set and volume per lift, and sets a target for your next session. Records collects every PR you've hit, and weekly volume shows which muscles are on target.

  • Exercise progression with next-session targets
  • Records: every PR, sorted by what's new
  • Weekly volume, strength details, cardio trend & monthly recap

Get the app

Install in 10 seconds, right from your browser.

NorseLift is a Progressive Web App: it installs from your browser, launches full-screen from your home screen and keeps working offline.

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open app.norselift.com in Safari
  2. Tap Share
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen

Android

  1. Open app.norselift.com in Chrome
  2. Tap ⋮ menu
  3. Tap Install app

Mac, Windows & Linux

  1. Open app.norselift.com
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar
  3. Or just keep it in a tab
