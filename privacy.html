Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data NorseLift collects, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what your rights are.

This policy follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) as it applies in Norway through the Personal Data Act (personopplysningsloven). Norwegian consumer protection rules also apply to you as a customer and take precedence over anything in our policies that would limit your mandatory rights.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is SNORRE AAS IT, a sole proprietorship (enkeltpersonforetak) owned by Snorre Aas, org.nr. 924 028 556, Hogstvetveien 3, 1435 Ås, Norway. Email: support@norselift.com.

2. What data we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address and profile photo URL (if you sign in with Google), your sign-in method, and when you created your account and were last active.
  • Training data: everything you log in the app, such as exercises, sets, reps, weights, cardio, workouts, programs, schedules, training blocks, personal records and body weight entries.
  • Subscription data: your Stripe customer and subscription IDs, plan, status, billing period and payment history. Card details are handled by Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.
  • Gift and referral data: your referral code, who referred whom and which rewards were given, and the name and email address of a person you send a Pro gift to.
  • Support data: the emails you send us and our replies.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device information and server logs created when you use the website or app.
  • Analytics data: page views and clicks on the website (Google Analytics, only if you accept), and cookieless, aggregated page-view statistics (Vercel Web Analytics).

Body weight and training data can say something about your health. We only use it to provide the features you use, you decide what you log, and you can delete it at any time.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account and training data: as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your account and workout data are deleted.
  • Subscription and payment records: for as long as the Bookkeeping Act (bokføringsloven) requires, normally 5 years after the end of the financial year, even if you delete your account.
  • Support emails: as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up.
  • Server logs: a short period, as set by our hosting provider.
  • Google Analytics data: no longer than 14 months. If you withdraw consent, the cookies on your device are deleted.
  • Your consent choice and theme preference: stored in your own browser until you clear it.

5. Who we share it with

We use these service providers (data processors) to run NorseLift. They may only use your data to provide their services to us.

  • Google (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore): sign-in and storage of your account and training data. Privacy
  • Stripe: payments and subscriptions. For some purposes, such as fraud prevention and meeting its legal obligations, Stripe acts as an independent controller. Privacy
  • Vercel: hosting of the website, app and server functions, and Vercel Web Analytics (cookieless). Privacy
  • Resend: sending service emails, such as Pro gift and referral reward emails. Privacy
  • Google Analytics 4: website analytics on norselift.com, only if you accept in the cookie banner. Privacy

We may also share data when the law requires it, for example with public authorities.

6. Transfers outside the EEA

Some of these providers are based in, or process data in, the United States or other countries outside the EEA. When this happens, the transfer is protected by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (for certified providers) and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, with additional safeguards where needed. Contact us if you want more information.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your data. You can delete your account in the app's Settings at any time. This removes your account and workout data and cancels any active Pro subscription. Payment records we must keep by law are kept as described above;
  • data portability: request a copy of your data by emailing support@norselift.com;
  • restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (for Google Analytics, use Cookie settings). This does not affect processing before you withdrew it.

Email support@norselift.com to use your rights. We reply within one month. We may need to confirm your identity first.

You also have the right to complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet. We would appreciate the chance to help first.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest with our providers, access to production systems is limited, and sign-in uses secure token-based sessions. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the authorities of a data breach when the law requires it.

10. Children

NorseLift is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you are under 18, you need permission from a parent or guardian to buy a subscription. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If we make significant changes, we will tell you by email or in the app before they take effect.
