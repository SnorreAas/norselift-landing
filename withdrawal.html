Right of withdrawal (angrerett)

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Norwegian consumer protection rules, including the Cancellation Act (angrerettloven) and, where applicable, the Consumer Purchases Act (forbrukerkjøpsloven) and the rules on digital services to consumers, apply to your purchase. If anything on this page conflicts with your mandatory rights, the law takes precedence.

Your right to withdraw

As a consumer, you can withdraw from your NorseLift Pro purchase within 14 days without giving a reason. The 14 days start on the day you subscribe (the day the contract is concluded).

When the right lapses: if, when you subscribe, you expressly ask us to give you Pro immediately and acknowledge that you thereby lose your right of withdrawal, the right lapses once Pro has been made available to you, to the extent angrerettloven allows. If the law instead gives you a right to withdraw with a deduction for the period you have used, we follow the law.

How to withdraw

To withdraw, send us a clear statement that you want to withdraw before the 14 days have passed, for example by email to support@norselift.com or by post. You can use the form below, but you don't have to. It is enough that you send your message before the deadline.

Please include the email address of your NorseLift account so we can find your subscription.

What happens when you withdraw

  • We refund the payments we have received from you for the purchase no later than 14 days after we receive your withdrawal.
  • The refund is made with the same payment method you used, unless you expressly agree otherwise. You are not charged any fees for the refund.
  • If you asked us to start Pro during the withdrawal period and the law gives you a right to withdraw with a deduction, you pay an amount proportionate to the period you had Pro before you told us you were withdrawing.
  • Your Pro subscription ends when the withdrawal is processed, and your account returns to the Free plan. Your workout data is not deleted.

Withdrawing is different from cancelling. If you only want to stop future renewals, you can cancel at any time; see Cancellation in our Terms of Service.
